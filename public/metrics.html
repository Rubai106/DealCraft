What each number means

  • Total records — every work record ever created, all statuses.
  • Confirmed / activation rate — records that reached mutual confirmation (ACTIVE, CLOSED or DISPUTED) ÷ total records.
  • ChangeLock jobs / rate — records with at least one approved ChangeLock (price or deadline change both sides accepted).
  • Dispute jobs / rate — records with at least one dispute opened.
  • Passport-qualified jobs — CLOSED records whose close was mutual (a hirer-only close does not qualify).
  • Avg SMS segments per record — segments actually sent for the activation summary SMS, per record that got one, counting both parties' messages (today: 3 segments × 2 recipients = 6 per record). The unit-economics number for the summary SMS.
